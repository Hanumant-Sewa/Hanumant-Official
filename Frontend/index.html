ABOUT US

Compassion in Action,
Service with Devotion.

HanumanSeva is a non-profit organization committed to ensuring that no one sleeps hungry. Inspired by the spirit of selfless service, we organize food distribution drives, community kitchens, volunteer programs, and hunger relief initiatives to bring hope and dignity to those in need.

Our Mission

To provide nutritious meals, inspire volunteerism, reduce food waste, and build a society where no one sleeps hungry.

Our Vision

A compassionate India where communities unite to end hunger and every individual receives food with dignity and respect.

Our Values

Compassion, transparency, integrity, dedication, teamwork and selfless service guide every initiative we undertake.

OUR SERVICES

Serving Society Through Meaningful Initiatives

Our work extends beyond providing meals. We strive to create lasting change by supporting vulnerable communities through hunger relief, volunteer programs, awareness campaigns, and emergency assistance.

Daily Food Distribution

Fresh and hygienic meals distributed to people facing hunger, ensuring dignity with every serving.

Disaster Relief

Emergency food kits and essentials delivered quickly during floods, heatwaves and natural disasters.

Food Donation Drives

Community food collection campaigns that encourage people to share meals with those in need.

Volunteer Programs

Join our dedicated volunteer network and become a part of every food distribution initiative.

Awareness Campaigns

Creating awareness about food waste, nutrition and the importance of helping underprivileged communities.

Community Support

Supporting orphanages, elderly homes and economically weaker families through regular outreach programs.
